## Profile

I am a **computational biologist and multi-omics data specialist** with a background in computer science, molecular biology, and computational genomics.

My work focuses on transforming complex biological datasets into meaningful scientific insights through **bioinformatics analysis, integrative approaches, and reproducible computational workflows**.

My research experience spans **plant molecular biology and human neurogenomics**, with expertise in transcriptomic, epigenomic, and spatial data analysis. I am particularly interested in applying computational approaches across diverse biological systems, organisms, and research areas.

As founder of **Arkhe Multiomics**, I provide specialized bioinformatics consulting and technical training for researchers, laboratories, and academic institutions.

**Open to international research collaborations, consulting projects, and specialized technical training.**

## Research Collaboration & Services

I collaborate with researchers and scientific teams seeking computational support for their omics research projects.

### Omics Data Analysis & Bioinformatics Consulting

Some services I provide include:

- **Bulk RNA-seq:** Differential expression, functional interpretation, and gene co-expression networks.
- **Single-cell & single-nucleus RNA-seq:** Quality control, clustering, cell-type annotation, and exploratory analysis.
- **Single-cell multiome:** Integrated analysis of gene expression and chromatin accessibility.
- **Spatial transcriptomics:** Spatial gene expression patterns and cellular organization.
- **Reproducible workflows:** Computational pipelines, documentation, and analytical best practices.

Projects can be tailored to specific datasets, research questions, and computational requirements.

### Specialized Technical Training

Through [Arkhe Multiomics](https://elarkhe.github.io), I develop and deliver **hands-on bioinformatics training** for research laboratories, universities, and scientific institutions.

Training activities include:

- Practical workshops using real biological datasets.
- Guided analysis with R/Python and bioinformatics tools.
- Single-cell, multiome, and spatial transcriptomics workflows.
- Training adapted to research teams and institutional needs.

The goal is to help researchers develop practical analytical skills and apply reproducible workflows to their own projects.

**Have an omics dataset, a research challenge, or a team seeking computational expertise?**

[Let's connect and explore opportunities for collaboration.](mailto:elarkhe@gmail.com)

## Research Interests

My research focuses on **computational biology and high-dimensional omics data analysis**, with two primary areas of interest:

- **Neuroscience & Disease:** Single-cell, single-nucleus, and spatial transcriptomics to investigate cellular heterogeneity and molecular organization in the human brain.
- **Plant–Pathogen Interactions:** Transcriptomics and gene co-expression networks to investigate plant defense mechanisms, with growing interest in single-cell and spatial transcriptomics.

I am particularly interested in integrative approaches combining transcriptomic, epigenomic, and spatial information to investigate complex biological systems.

## Technical Expertise

My computational experience includes the analysis of transcriptomic, epigenomic, and spatial omics datasets using R, Python, and high-performance computing environments.

### Core Bioinformatics Ecosystem

- **Programming & environments:** R, Python, Bash/Shell, Linux, HPC (SLURM/SGE), Git/GitHub.
- **Single-cell & spatial analysis:** Seurat, Signac, SpatialExperiment, spatialLIBD, scran, scater, bluster.
- **Transcriptomics & network analysis:** WGCNA, DESeq2, ggplot2, tidyverse, ComplexHeatmap.
- **10x Genomics pipelines:** Cell Ranger, Cell Ranger ARC, Space Ranger.
- **RNA-seq preprocessing & quantification:** FastQC, Trimmomatic, STAR, HISAT2, Salmon, Kallisto, HTSeq.

I prioritize **reproducibility, biological interpretation, and clear documentation**, adapting computational approaches to the scientific questions and characteristics of each dataset.

## Research Background & Experience

My background combines **computer science, molecular biology, and computational genomics**, with research experience spanning plant biology and human neuroscience.

During my PhD at the **Centro de Investigación Científica de Yucatán (CICY)**, I investigated plant defense mechanisms using bulk RNA-seq and gene co-expression network analysis.

At the **Lieber Institute for Brain Development (LIBD)**, I contributed to collaborative neurogenomics research integrating **single-nucleus RNA-seq, chromatin accessibility, and spatial transcriptomics** to investigate cellular and molecular organization in the human brain.

Today, as founder of **[Arkhe Multiomics](https://elarkhe.github.io)**, I apply this interdisciplinary experience to support research teams through **omics data analysis, bioinformatics consulting, and specialized technical training**.
