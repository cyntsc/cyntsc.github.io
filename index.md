Hi,

I’m a data scientist and multi-omics specialist who loves bringing together biology, statistics, and computer science to address complex biological questions.

As the Founder of El Arkhe, I lead two complementary initiatives:

*Arkhe Multiomics*: focused on advanced training and methodological programs in omics technologies. We collaborate with academic and private research groups to strengthen analytical capacity and support scalable research infrastructure.

*Arkhe Cuentos Moleculares*: an educational and cultural platform dedicated to the omics sciences through multiple languages. We work to communicate molecular biology concepts in an accessible, jargon-free way, contributing to the broader cultural understanding of science.

I am open to scientific collaboration and strategic program development.

Thank you for visiting my website.
